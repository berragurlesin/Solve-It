# Solve It
Hi everyone, 
a month ago I was at an entrepreneurship camp, and we often had to find a problem to solve. That’s because one of the most important aspects of entrepreneurship is creating products that offer solutions to the problems people face in their daily lives, you shouldn't just invent a problem. The biggest problem we faced was that **we couldn’t find a problem to solve!** And on the one hand, there are already many people facing problems, and they too are looking for solutions to their problems. We kept asking people about the issues they were facing, and I thought to myself, *Why not do something about this?*

## What is the Problem and How Do I *Solve It*?

Millions of people face problems every day and are actively seeking solutions. They either share their problems with people close to them or post them on platforms like Reddit, Quora, Nextdoor, and the other forums. However, on these existing channels critical issues gets lost among the other comments. It can take months or even years for responses to come, or the problems may never be resolved. It’s impossible to track which problem is urgent, who is working on it, or what is the solution. 

This situation make things complicated. On the one hand, entrepreneurs or people like me who want to help others cannot be aware of the problems people face in their daily lives unless they are directly involved. On the other hand, people who share their problems dont have a system which they can receive real solutions. In short, while people who want to find solutions cannot easily identify real problems, people facing problems struggle to find real help.

This is exactly where Solve It comes in. Solve It transforms every issue into a structured format that categorizes problems and shows their level of urgency. So, it offers people a space to share their issues and entrepreneurs to share real solutions, and solve the both sides problem.

## Features

* **Registration and Login:** Users can create accounts and log in.
* **User Profiles:** Users activity history (their issues and solutions) can be viewed on this profile page.
* **“My Projects” Tab:** Users can track their published issues or solution suggestions from this page.
* **Real-Time Notifications:** Users receive notifications when a new solution is added to their ticket or a comment is posted.
* **Search Bar:** Users can search through tickets by keywords, titles etc.
* **Urgency and Category Filtering:** When searching for issues, they can be categorized by subject and urgency. Problems with published solutions are also in a separate page and can be filtered.
* **“Same Issue” Button:** Users can click this button to say that they experience the same issue. This allows to filter the common problems.
* **Publishing Issues:** Users can directly publish the issues they’re experiencing, select a category, and specify the urgency level. They can also delete the issues they’ve created.
* **Publishing Solutions and Guides:** Users can publish clear solution readme's for the tickets. They can also delete the solutions they’ve published if they wish.
* **Ticket Reservation:** People can reserve tickets to say that they are gonna work on that issue, this way not so many people works on same issue.
* **Image Upload and Optimization:** Users can attach images when publishing a problem or solution. Uploaded images are automatically resized and compressed on the browser side (using Canvas), this way there will be no issues with speed etc.

## What Did I Use?

* **Frontend:** HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+)
* **UI & Styling:** Custom CSS modals, responsive layouts
* **Client-Side Processing:** HTML5 canvas API (image optimization and downscaling)
* **Data & Media Handling:** Base64 encoding for assets and attachments

## How to Run it Locally?

If you want to install Solve It on your own computer (I don't know what you are gonna do this for but, you might wanna take a look)

1. Clone or download this repository to your computer
2. Open the project folder
3. Open `index.html` directly in any web browser (or use the live server extension in VS Code)
