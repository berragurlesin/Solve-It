Hi everyonee, 
Over the past few months, I was at an entrepreneurship camp, and we often had to find a problem to solve. That’s because one of the most important aspects of entrepreneurship is creating products that offer solutions to the problems people face in their daily lives. The biggest problem we faced, however, was that **we couldn’t find a problem to solve!** We kept asking people about the issues they were facing, and I thought to myself, Why not do something about this? 

Solve It is a platform where people facing problems can share their issues and what they want resolved, while those eager to come up with solutions can share their ideas. In this README, I’ll explain everything about my project.
