# Solve It
Hi everyone, 
a month ago, I was at an entrepreneurship camp, and we often had to find a problem to solve. That’s because one of the most important aspects of entrepreneurship is creating products that offer solutions to the problems people face in their daily lives. The biggest problem we faced, however, was that **we couldn’t find a problem to solve!** And on the one hand, there are already many people facing problems, and they, too, are looking for solutions to their problems. We kept asking people about the issues they were facing, and I thought to myself, *Why not do something about this?*

## What is the Problem and How Do We *Solve It*?

Millions of people face problems every day and are actively seeking solutions. They either share these problems with those close to them or post them on platforms like Reddit, Quora, Nextdoor, and traditional forums. However, on these existing channels, critical issues get lost amid scattered text and disorganized comment threads. It can take months or even years for responses to come, or the problems may never be resolved. It’s impossible to track which problem is urgent, who is working on it, or how it was ultimately resolved. 

This situation creates a clear disconnect. On the one hand, entrepreneurs or people like me who want to help others cannot be aware of the problems people face in their daily lives unless they are directly involved. On the other hand, individuals who voice their problems lack a structured system through which they can receive clear and actionable responses. In short, while those who want to find solutions cannot easily identify real problems, those facing problems struggle to find help that can be turned into action.

This is exactly where Solve It comes in. The platform transforms every problem within the community and in daily life into a “ticket” structure that is transparent, categorized, and highlights the level of urgency. In this way, it facilitates problem tracking by providing individuals with a dedicated space to voice their concerns, while also enabling developers, entrepreneurs, and problem solvers to publish step-by-step guides within this clean and trackable system, bringing both sides together in a structured framework.

## Features

* **User Registration and Login:** Users can create accounts and log in.
* **User Profiles:** The activity history of users who create tickets and publish solution guides can be viewed on their profile page.
* **“My Projects” Tab:** Users can track their previously published issues or solution suggestions from this page.
* **Real-Time Notifications:** Users receive instant notifications when a new solution is added to their ticket, the status is updated, or a comment is posted.
* **Search Bar:** Users can instantly search through tickets by keywords, titles, and categories.
* **Urgency and Category Filtering:** When searching for issues, they can be categorized by subject and urgency; this allows those providing solutions to quickly focus on high-priority issues. Problems with published solutions are also directed to a separate page and can be filtered.
* **“Same Issue” Button:** Users can indicate with a single click that they are experiencing the same issue; this allows the most common problems to be filtered.
* **Interactive Ticket Windows:** Provides a pop-up window interface for reviewing issue details, reading descriptions, and submitting solutions.
* **Publishing and Detailing Issues:** Users can directly publish the issues they’re experiencing, select a category, and specify the urgency level. This allows them to customize the issue. Users can delete the issues they’ve created.
* **Providing Solutions and Publishing Guides:** Solution providers can publish clear, step-by-step, actionable solution guides under tickets, free from confusion. They can add a README. Users can delete the solutions they’ve published if they wish.
* **Ticket Reservation & Workload Balancing:** Allows problem-solvers to reserve dynamic tickets to indicate they are actively working on them, preventing duplicate efforts and encouraging the resolution of diverse issues.
* **Image Upload and In-Browser Image Optimization:** Users can attach images when publishing a problem or solution. Uploaded images are automatically resized and compressed on the browser side (using Canvas), ensuring fast loading without compromising page performance.
* **Dynamic Comments and File Uploads:** Provides a seamless infrastructure for adding solution notes to tickets and attaching files/images in Base64 format.

3. Open `index.html` directly in any web browser (or use the *Live Server* extension in VS Code).

## Notes on Logo and Concept Design

The platform’s visual identity and concept were shaped around the “ticket” theme rather than classic forum posts. I chose this theme to enhance the desire to use the platform aesthetically, give it a unique look, and create a format that’s easy to follow—thereby highlighting a sense of progress and ownership. The dark theme and high-contrast accent colors used in the color palette provide a modern and professional interface that is easy on the eyes.

The intricate line structure between the words “Solve” and “It” in the logotype design symbolizes a problem waiting to be solved, emphasizing the app’s core vision. Similarly, those intricate lines from the logo also appear in the favicon.

## Future Roadmap

Although I’ve completed the platform’s MVP phase for now, there are many features that come to mind and could be added in the future—even more than what I’ve listed here!

* **Welcome and Sign-Up Screen:** Design a sleeker, standalone “Sign In / Sign Up” page to greet users before they proceed to the main platform.
* **Interactive User Guide:** Provide a step-by-step guide introducing the platform’s basic features to first-time visitors.
* **“Torn Ticket” Design for Resolved Issues:** Visually convey that the problem has been resolved by transforming the image of resolved tickets into a “cut/torn ticket” effect.
* **Mobile App Compatibility:** Make the platform fully compatible with mobile devices or develop a mobile app version.
* **Solution Rating, Badge Systems, and Monetization:** Enable users to rate the solutions provided and allow contributors to earn points and badges; develop revenue models to ensure the platform’s sustainability.
