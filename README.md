# Solve It
A month ago, I was at an entrepreneurship camp and we often had to find a problem to solve (that's because solving *real problems* is important when it comes to entrepreneurship, you shouldn't invent a new one) But, the biggest problem we faced was that **we couldn't find a problem to solve!** And I thought for a sec, there are already so many people facing problems and looking for solutions. We were trying to ask people to find a idea, then I thought to myself **Why not do something about this?**

## What is the Problem and How Do I *Solve It?*
Millions of people face problems everyday as I said and they are looking for solutions, mostly on social media or forums. However on these platforms important issues gets lost among the million other. It can take months, even years for responses to come, or the problems may never be resolved. It's nearly impossable to track which ones are urgent, who is working on them or what is the solution.

This makes things complicated. On the one hand, entrepreneurs or people like me who want to help others cannot learn the real problems people face unless they are directly involved. On the other hand, people who have problems don't have a system where they can share, and receive good solutions. In short, while people who want to solve something cannot easily identify real problems, people facing problems struggle to find real help.

This is where Solve It comes in! Solve It categorizes issues in a structured format and offers people a platform to share their issues and entrepreneurs to share soltions, and solve both sides problem.
